## What We Do

We build and release open source tools for machine learning — from data collection and annotation pipelines to inference utilities and dataset schemas, across both **vision** and **language**. Everything we build is free to use, fork, and contribute to.

- **Computer vision** — detection, classification, segmentation, and real-time inference pipelines
- **Language & text** — annotated datasets, evaluation diagnostics, and annotation tooling for NLP
- **Data collection** — tools and scripts for sourcing and capturing visual and textual data
- **Data annotation** — open utilities for labeling, reviewing, and exporting training datasets in any format

We are not a company or a service provider. We are a lab — a group building in the open because we believe better tooling should be accessible to everyone, not locked behind enterprise contracts.

---

## Our Philosophy

Most ML tooling is either fragmented, paywalled, or buried inside closed platforms. We think the community deserves solid, well-documented, composable tools that just work — whether the data is pixels or sentences.

Everything here is open source. Use it in your research, your projects, your products. If something is broken or missing, open an issue or send a PR.
